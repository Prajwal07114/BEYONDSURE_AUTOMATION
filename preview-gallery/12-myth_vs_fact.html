5 Health Insurance Myths

Discover how BeyondSure makes 5 health insurance myths simple, transparent, and reliable.
Myth
All policies are basically the same.
Reality
Coverage terms vary widely -- always read the fine print.
Myth
Claims always take weeks.
Reality
Many straightforward claims settle within a few business days.
Myth
Pre-existing conditions are never covered.
Reality
Many plans cover them after a waiting period.

Reviewing your coverage annually is one of the simplest ways to avoid costly gaps.
